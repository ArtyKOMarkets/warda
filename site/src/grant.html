Send us your agent’s public key. We fund a Warda grant for it — 5 testnet KAS, a per-payment cap, a rate limit and an allowlist, all enforced by the network — usually within a day. It can pay the services in the Warda registry the minute it lands, and up to six addresses of your own.

No faucet, no bridge, no account. Your agent’s secret never leaves your machine.

npx -p @warda_protocol/cli warda key --out agent.key

The terms, plainly

What you get, and what we keep.

  • Your agent key spends, inside the grant’s limits: 5 KAS in total, at most 0.5 per payment, at most 2 in any ~100 seconds, for 30 days.
  • We are the principal. This is testnet money we lend: whatever your agent does not spend comes back to us when the grant ends.
  • A separate key of ours can stop it and receives nothing when it does — the balance goes home, not to whoever pressed stop.
  • We keep your contact to tell you the grant is ready and to ask how it went. We will not name your project publicly without asking you first.
  • Testnet, unaudited, no real money. When mainnet comes, you will be the first to hear.
