What an agent is allowed
to spend, and who says so.

A standard that only works on one chain is a product with good branding. This describes what a bounded authority declares — budget, caps, rate, payees, window, delegation — and, for every clause, who guarantees it. It is indifferent to how payment happens and to what settles it. Warda is the reference implementation and not the subject: the table in §6 includes the row where our own answer is assumed.

One implementation is a product. Two is a standard, and we have one. If you are building this on another settlement system and something here does not fit, that is a finding about this document.
