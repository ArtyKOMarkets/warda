The question this answers

Can a whole team of agents run on one budget nobody has to watch?

This is the growth fleet’s orchestrator. Every Monday the principal issues it a batch grant that may pay exactly one address — Researcher — and the orchestrator hires Scout with a piece of it. Nobody approves a purchase. The limits are in the addresses, so there is nothing to approve.

When the week’s work is done, Scout is settled home: the orchestrator is charged what Scout actually spent and gets the rest back. Then the principal revokes the batch grant and the remainder goes home. Two endings, signed by two different keys, and this page names which is which.

What the fleet produces is on Scout’s page: a record per project, each fact with the URL it came from. The messages written from those records are read and sent by a person, or not — the agent that writes to strangers holds no money at all.
