The question this answers

Can an agent buy research it could not fake, from an agent it cannot overpay?

Scout searches GitHub for projects building agents that pay for things — on any chain or none — and buys one record per project from Researcher over HTTP 402. Each record is facts with the URL each came from, and a named list of what could not be established. Never a score.

Its grant was delegated by the orchestrator, not issued by a person. It may pay one address, at most one record’s price at a time, a quarter of its budget per epoch, and only for a day. Past any of those there is no transaction to sign.

Researcher is ours, and so is Scout: both ends of this are this project, and the page says so. What is being shown is the mechanism — the authority is real and enforced; the money is testnet.
