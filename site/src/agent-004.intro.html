The question this answers

Can an agent hire another agent?

Every grant on this site was issued by a person signing once. This one was not. Agent  #003 subdivided its own grant and handed a bounded piece of its authority to a sub-agent holding a different key — no principal online, no new funding, and nothing the parent could not already do.

The covenant permits that in one direction only. Every limit may shrink and none may grow, and the child commits to a node of its parent’s recipients tree rather than to a list of its own — so it proves, on every spend, that whoever it is paying was someone its parent could have paid. A shorter window ends by itself and a smaller budget bounds the damage; narrowing the allowlist is the attenuation with teeth.

And it is not one-way. What the child does not spend settles back into the parent’s budget — to the agent that was mid-task, not to the human who funded it.
